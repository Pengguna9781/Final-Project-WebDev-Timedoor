Hero of the People

J. Jonah Jameson is one of New York's most influential journalists, a self-made publisher, and the longtime owner of the Daily Bugle. Starting his career from the bottom, Jameson worked his way from paperboy to reporter and eventually built the Bugle into one of the city's major newspapers. Throughout his career, he has investigated corruption, confronted criminals, supported civil rights, and repeatedly risked his own safety to expose wrongdoing. His most controversial campaign has been his criticism of Spider-Man. While others were quick to celebrate the masked vigilante, Jameson asked a simple question: Who holds someone with that much power accountable? His critics call him sensationalist. His supporters call him fearless. The truth is that Jameson has never been afraid to challenge popular opinion—even when that opinion is wrong. He may shout. He may exaggerate. He may have a rather unhealthy obsession with Spider-Man. But beneath the headlines is a journalist who genuinely believes that New York deserves the truth. And J. Jonah Jameson intends to make sure it gets it.
